Machine translation is literal, and literal is exactly wrong for songs. Translate a line of Urdu poetry word for word and you get a grammatical sentence that means almost nothing.

The version worth building is the one a friend who speaks the language would give you: what it says, then what it means.

## Keeping three columns aligned

If the model merges or drops a single line, the original, translation and meaning columns stop matching. So the response format has to be constrained rather than free prose.
