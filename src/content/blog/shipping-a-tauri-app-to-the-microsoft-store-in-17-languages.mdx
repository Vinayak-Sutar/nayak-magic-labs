A working build is maybe half of a release. The rest is packaging, a store listing that explains the app in a few seconds, translations, and a way to find out what breaks on machines you will never see.

## The shape of the app

Valocut is a Tauri shell around a Next.js interface, with a local FastAPI sidecar doing the heavy work. Nothing leaves the machine, so the architecture is designed around making a desktop CPU feel fast rather than around scaling a server.

## After launch

Sentry reports crashes and PostHog shows which features people actually use. Both shape what gets built next more than any roadmap does.
